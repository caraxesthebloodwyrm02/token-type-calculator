# The Waterfall Letter

Regarding the dream of the large waterfall...

It is modeled after a real area in Japan, likely the **Nachi Falls** in the Wakayama Prefecture (Kansai region, the real-world inspiration for Johto). In the world of Johto, this resonance is felt near **Tohjo Falls** or the silver peaks.

The resonance observed in the `token-type-calculator` lab suggests that the "Spatial" and "Rhythm" tokens converge here. When you pointed the device, you weren't just scanning code; you were scanning a memory of a place that exists both in the dream and the strata.
